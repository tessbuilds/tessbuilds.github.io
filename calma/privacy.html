Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026  ·  tessbuild

🔒 The short version: Your mood history and journal are stored on your device. Calma has no accounts, advertising, or third-party tracking. AI features process only the information needed for the response, and journal notes are included only with your permission. We do not sell your data.

1. Who we are

Calma is an iOS app developed by tessbuild, an independent developer. You can reach us at support@tessbuild.com.

2. Information stored on your device

Calma stores your core wellness history locally on your device. This includes mood scores, journal notes, check-in timestamps, streaks, coping activity history and feedback, reminder settings, AI consent choices, and breathing cue preferences. Calma does not require an account and does not provide cloud sync.

Calma may also store a metadata-only summary of your most recent AI request, such as the feature used, categories of information sent, and date. It does not store the actual journal text in this audit summary.

3. Information processed for AI features

When you use an AI-powered feature, Calma sends limited information over an encrypted connection to our backend service so a response can be generated:

  • Journal prompts: the selected mood score
  • Breathing recommendations: the latest mood score
  • Coping suggestions: the current mood score and, only if allowed, the current journal note
  • Insights: recent mood scores and, only if allowed, journal notes within the Insights window

Calma may prefetch score-only breathing or Insights responses to reduce loading time. Journal notes are never included in this background prefetch. You can change journal-note permission at any time in Settings.

Calma does not send the following information to its AI service:

  • Your name or email address
  • Subscription or payment information
  • Notification history
  • Local coping feedback and helpfulness outcomes
  • Location, contacts, photos, or biometric information
  • Mood history outside the data needed for the requested feature

4. How information is used

Information processed by Calma is used only to:

  • Provide mood tracking, journaling, breathing, coping, and Insights features
  • Generate the AI response you requested or a score-only response prefetched for faster loading
  • Maintain subscriptions and restore purchases through Apple
  • Protect, troubleshoot, and operate the service

We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or use it to build advertising profiles.

5. Service providers

Calma relies on the following service providers:

  • Amazon Web Services (AWS): API Gateway and Lambda provide backend infrastructure for AI requests.
  • Anthropic: processes the limited request content needed to generate AI responses.
  • Apple: distributes the app and manages subscriptions, payments, purchase history, and restoration through the App Store and StoreKit.

These providers may process information in accordance with their own terms and privacy policies. Calma does not integrate advertising networks or third-party behavioral analytics SDKs.

6. Notifications

If you enable daily check-in reminders, Calma uses iOS's local notification system. These notifications are scheduled entirely on your device. We do not use push notification servers and have no visibility into whether notifications are delivered or opened.

7. Data retention and deletion

Your mood history, journal notes, preferences, and local personalization remain on your device until you delete them. You can use Settings → Delete All Data to remove Calma's local data and reset the app, or delete the app from your iPhone.

Calma's backend does not create user accounts or intentionally maintain a server-side mood or journal history. Information sent for an AI request may be processed and retained for limited periods by infrastructure and AI service providers according to their applicable policies and operational requirements. Deleting local data cannot recall information that has already been processed in a prior request.

8. Security and international processing

Calma uses encrypted HTTPS connections for network requests and limits the information included in each AI request. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

AWS, Anthropic, and Apple may process information in the United States or other countries where they operate. Those locations may have different data-protection laws from your country.

9. Children's privacy

Calma is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children. If you believe a child has used the app, please contact us and we will provide guidance.

10. Medical disclaimer

Calma is not a medical or mental health service. It is designed for personal reflection and general wellness only. Calma does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. If you are experiencing a mental health crisis or emergency, please contact a qualified healthcare provider or emergency services immediately.

11. Changes to this policy

If we make changes to this privacy policy, we will update the "last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

12. Contact

Questions about this privacy policy? Email us at support@tessbuild.com. We'll respond within 48 hours.

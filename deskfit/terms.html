AI-Generated Content

AI generation is optional, requires your permission to share the preferences described in our Privacy Policy, and requires connectivity and service availability. You can change this choice in Settings. Local routines and saved-workout replay do not require a new AI request. If AI generation is unavailable, the App may offer a ready-to-use local routine.

When you request AI-generated workouts, the plans are produced using artificial intelligence. While we strive for quality and safety, AI-generated content may occasionally contain errors, inappropriate suggestions, or plans that are not suitable for your specific condition.

tessbuild makes no warranty that AI-generated workout plans will be accurate, complete, or appropriate for your individual needs.

Repeating a saved routine preserves its saved exercises, order, instructions, sets, reps, and rest times; it does not reassess your current condition or generate updated advice. Choose a routine suitable for how you feel, use a comfortable range of movement, and stop if you feel pain, dizziness, or discomfort.

Saved routines and history are stored on your device. The latest 50 replayable routines are retained without a fixed time expiry, and up to 10 Favorites are protected from automatic cleanup. Older non-favorite routine details may be removed while completion history remains. Each completed repeat is recorded separately. Earlier history entries may not have recoverable details for replay.

You can use Delete Workout, Clear History, or Delete All Data to remove the corresponding local data. These actions cannot be undone through the App. Clear History keeps your profile and settings; Reset Onboarding does not delete workout history. Deleting local data does not remove records already written to Apple Health, copies in existing device backups, or files you exported or shared to another destination. The App does not provide account-based cloud synchronization or guarantee recovery of deleted local data.

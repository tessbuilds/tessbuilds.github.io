Privacy Policy for MisTap

Effective date: September 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the mobile application "MisTap" (the "App"), published by tessbuilds, handles information.

Summary MisTap does not collect personal information through its own servers or databases. The App has no advertising, no analytics, no third-party tracking SDKs, no subscriptions, and no MisTap account system. Gameplay data and preferences are stored locally on your device. MisTap uses Apple Game Center for leaderboards, which is an Apple-provided service.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

MisTap does not collect:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or mailing address.
  • Your precise or approximate location.
  • Your contacts, photos, camera, or microphone data.
  • Your health or fitness information.
  • Advertising identifiers for advertising or tracking.
  • Your browsing history or search history.

MisTap does not include third-party advertising, analytics, attribution, or tracking SDKs. tessbuilds does not sell personal information or use information from MisTap to track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

MisTap stores gameplay information and preferences locally on your device using Apple's standard local storage technologies. This may include:

  • High scores and game progress.
  • Armor and streak-related gameplay state.
  • Total runs and other gameplay state.
  • Haptic, sound, and colorblind-mode preferences.
  • Onboarding status.
  • Daily Challenge history.

This locally stored information is not transmitted to tessbuilds. Deleting the App generally removes information stored locally by the App. Information separately maintained by Apple services, such as Game Center, is managed by Apple.

3. Apple Game Center

MisTap uses Apple Game Center to provide leaderboard functionality for Classic, Chaos, and Daily Challenge modes.

When you choose to use Game Center features, information such as your leaderboard scores, Game Center player identity, rankings, and related Game Center activity may be handled by Apple through its Game Center service.

tessbuilds does not operate a separate MisTap account system and does not collect Game Center information into its own servers or databases. We do not receive your Apple ID password or credentials.

Game Center is provided and operated by Apple. Your use of Game Center is subject to Apple's applicable terms and privacy practices. You can learn more at Apple's Privacy Policy .

4. Advertising, Analytics, and Tracking

MisTap does not display advertisements and does not contain third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

MisTap does not use your information for cross-app or cross-website tracking.

5. In-App Purchases and Subscriptions

MisTap does not currently offer subscriptions or in-app purchases.

6. Permissions

MisTap does not request access to:

  • Your camera or microphone.
  • Your photo library or contacts.
  • Your location.
  • Your health data.
  • Your motion data.
  • Your Bluetooth devices or local network.

Game Center functionality is provided through Apple's Game Center services when those features are used.

7. Children's Privacy

MisTap does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. The App does not require users to create a MisTap account or provide their name, email address, phone number, or other contact information.

Game Center is provided by Apple and is subject to Apple's applicable account requirements, parental controls, terms, and privacy practices.

8. Data Security

MisTap does not operate a server-side database containing MisTap user accounts or player profiles. Gameplay information stored by the App remains locally on the user's device except when an Apple-provided service, such as Game Center, is used.

Information handled by Apple services is subject to Apple's security and privacy practices.

9. Third-Party Services

MisTap uses operating-system services provided by Apple, including Game Center. These services are governed by Apple's applicable terms and privacy practices.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when MisTap's features or privacy practices change. When we make changes, we will update the effective date shown at the top of this page.

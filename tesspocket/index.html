Bank connections, with your permission

Bank linking is being prepared for launch using Plaid. When available, you choose which accounts to connect so TessPocket can import account details, balances, and transactions. Pre-release builds currently use Plaid Sandbox and simulated banking data.

Connecting a bank is optional. TessPocket is for adults aged 18 and over, including manual tracking and demo use.

Understand what is stored

TessPocket uses online accounts. Account and saved financial data are processed on AWS servers in the United States; this is not an on-device-only service. You can disconnect linked banks, delete their saved history, or request deletion of your TessPocket profile in the app.

Read the TessPocket privacy policy and terms of service before creating an account.

Account emails

We email a verification code when you sign up and a recovery code when you request a password reset. Codes are entered in the app; support will not ask you to send us your code.

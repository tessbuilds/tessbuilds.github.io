TessPocket · Privacy

Privacy policy

Effective and last updated: November 20, 2026

In brief: TessPocket uses online accounts and stores saved financial information on AWS servers in the United States. Bank linking is optional. We do not sell your personal data or use it for advertising. Disconnecting a bank preserves saved history unless you also choose to delete it.

This policy applies specifically to TessPocket and the TessPocket section of tessbuild.com. TessPocket is operated by tessbuild, based in Dallas, Texas, United States. Other tessbuild apps have their own applicable policies. Questions and privacy requests can be sent to support@tessbuild.com.

TessPocket is currently pre-release. Banking connections in current test builds use Plaid Sandbox and simulated data. The disclosures below explain current account and manual-data handling and the planned handling of user-authorized bank data when production bank linking becomes available. We will review these disclosures against the deployed service before public launch.

1. Information we handle

  • Account and authentication information: your email address, account identifiers, optional profile details, verification and recovery requests, session credentials, and authenticator enrollment information when used. Amazon Cognito processes passwords; we do not store your password in TessPocket’s application database. During password reset, our backend forwards your replacement password to Cognito.
  • Information you enter: manual accounts, balances, budgets, bills, payday plans, transaction categories, and related financial-planning information you choose to save. Saved account and planning data are sent to our backend and stored in its database.
  • Bank information you authorize: when bank linking is enabled, Plaid provides identifiers, institution and account names, account types, currencies, balances, short account-number masks, and transaction details such as dates, amounts, descriptions, and categories. We store Plaid connection credentials and imported data to provide your account history and financial views. Our integration does not request full account or routing numbers.
  • Device preferences: saved-account labels, masked email labels, preferences, and deletion-status references may be stored on your device. Optional biometric sign-in stores a refresh token in the device Keychain. Face ID and Touch ID are handled by Apple; TessPocket does not receive your facial or fingerprint templates.
  • Optional pay-stub reading: selected images and PDFs are read on your device using Apple’s document and text-recognition frameworks to populate calculator fields. The current pay-stub reader does not upload the selected file to TessPocket’s servers. Calculator inputs and estimates are separate from bank transaction data.
  • Support and operations: messages you send us and information needed to operate and protect the service, such as network addresses, request metadata, delivery events, and error diagnostics. Requests to this website also pass through its hosting provider.

2. How we use information

We use information to create and protect accounts, verify email ownership, recover access, retrieve authorized bank information, display balances and transaction history, categorize spending, calculate budget and cash-flow views, carry out deletion requests, provide support, and maintain service reliability and security. We do not sell or license your financial data, share it with advertisers, or use it for advertising targeting.

A real bank connection is optional. Demo use involves simulated banking data, but account information and anything you enter yourself are still handled as described here. Only connect accounts you are authorized to access.

3. Service providers and disclosures

  • Amazon Web Services (AWS): hosts our backend, database, authentication, secret storage, and transactional email services. Current TessPocket backend resources are in the United States. See AWS’s privacy notice.
  • Plaid: connects accounts with your authorization when bank linking is available. Plaid separately handles information used in its connection flow under the Plaid End User Privacy Policy. Any credentials entered into a bank or Plaid-hosted flow are handled by those services; TessPocket receives connection credentials rather than your bank login password.
  • Website and support providers: GitHub Pages hosts these pages, and our email provider processes support correspondence. These services process information necessary to deliver their functions. See GitHub’s privacy statement.
  • Disclosures where necessary: we may disclose information to comply with applicable law or valid legal process, investigate misuse, protect users and the service, or carry out an action you request.

This TessPocket website section does not include advertising or analytics scripts. Following an external link takes you to another service with its own practices.

4. Retention and deletion

  • Saved financial data: retained while your profile exists unless you remove it using the applicable controls. Historical and disconnected account transactions have no automatic expiry and remain available until you delete their saved history or your profile.
  • Disconnecting a bank: stops that connection. It does not close your bank account and does not erase saved TessPocket history unless you choose the history-deletion option. You can also delete a disconnected bank’s saved history in Linked banks.
  • Deleting your profile: use the in-app Delete account control. After identity verification, the service disconnects linked banks, deletes the authentication account, and removes the profile and associated financial records. If an upstream provider is unavailable, processing is retried; you can check the deletion status in the app. Deleting the app from your phone is not a profile-deletion request.
  • Limited deletion records: we retain a hashed identity fingerprint and deletion-status records to acknowledge completed deletion and prevent unintended recreation through stale credentials. These records are not the deleted transaction history.
  • Operational copies: the current pre-release backend keeps service logs for 14 days and automated database backups for seven days. Deletion from the active database does not immediately erase existing backup copies; they expire through normal retention. Support correspondence and records needed for security, dispute resolution, or legal obligations may be retained for those purposes.

Plaid and other providers apply their own retention and deletion rules. Contact us if you need help exercising a deletion or access request.

5. Your choices

You can review your saved records, edit supported information, export available posted transactions, disconnect banks, delete bank history, remove manual accounts, reset demo data, and delete your profile through the app. Exported files or information you share with another app are controlled by you and that destination. Contact support@tessbuild.com for access, correction, deletion, or other privacy requests; we may need to verify your identity before acting. Additional rights may apply depending on where you live.

6. Security

We use HTTPS for service communications, encrypted database storage, access controls that scope records to the signed-in profile, and additional application-level encryption for Plaid connection credentials and authenticator secrets. Optional saved biometric sign-in uses protected device Keychain storage. These measures reduce risk but cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Adults only

TessPocket, including manual tracking and demo use, is intended for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18. If you believe a minor has created a profile or provided information, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

8. Changes and contact

We will update this page and its date when our practices change. Where a change requires notice or consent, we will provide it through an appropriate channel before applying that change. For questions, contact tessbuild, Dallas, Texas, at support@tessbuild.com.
